## Security / integrity
| Concern | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| New input | Variant names trimmed, 1–40 chars, unique per product; prices whole non-negative Rupiah; max 20 variants — validated in `saveProduct`, not only the form. |
| Price integrity | A variant without its own takeaway price uses its own full price, never the product's takeaway price (tested), so a cheap override cannot leak across variants. Sale lines are snapshots; editing or deleting a variant never changes past sales. |
| Rename | Purely presentational; stored data, sync tables, backups and RLS unchanged. |
