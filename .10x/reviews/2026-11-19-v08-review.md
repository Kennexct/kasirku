Found and fixed during review
- Deleting a mode that an unpaid saved order used would have re-priced the debt on resume → delete is now refused until those orders are settled.
- A cart kept marked-up prices after its mode was deleted → cart re-prices to the default mode.
- Sales paid with a removed method could not be filtered in history → removed ones are listed as "(dihapus)".
- "Ingatkan via WhatsApp" wrapped on phones → actions stack on narrow screens; the generic chat button is hidden while there is something unpaid.

Not covered
- 12 methods / 8 modes on a 360 px phone (layout scrolls by design; not screenshot-tested at the limit).
- WhatsApp actually opening with the prepared text on a real phone (link format only).

## Security / privacy
| Concern | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Who can change methods and modes | Settings stays behind the owner gate; rules are enforced on the device only (same limit as ADR-003/004). |
| Sold-out switch | Open to any seller by design. A cashier can mark items sold out; nothing is deleted and no money moves. Not audited — acceptable for now, noted as debt. |
| User-typed names in exports | Method and mode names are now typed by the owner; they pass through the existing cell-level formula guard in CSV and XLSX (verified `sanitizeText` wraps every text cell). |
| Reminder message | Built from store name, customer name, order labels and amounts; URL-encoded; number from the canonical phone digits. No other customer data leaves the device, and nothing is sent until the seller presses send in WhatsApp. |
| Debt visibility | Any seller on the device can see who owes what — same exposure as the customer list (accepted in the customers review). |
| History integrity | Deletes are soft; orders keep method and mode names as snapshots, so reports and receipts never change after a removal. |
