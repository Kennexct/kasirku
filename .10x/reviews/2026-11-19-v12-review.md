Found and fixed
- Reopening a line that was entered by money amount and saving it unchanged would have re-rounded the weight and moved the total by a few Rupiah → the exact quantity is kept unless a different weight is typed.
- Unit names wrapped inside three narrow buttons on phones → two columns.

Not covered
- Units other than kg in the browser (table-driven; kg, g and L covered by unit tests).
- A measured product with variants (variant sheet then entry sheet) — code path exercised only by hand reasoning.
- English screenshots of the entry sheet (strings covered by the dictionary check and the leftover scan of the main screens).

## Security / integrity
| Concern | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Undercharging on flexible products | Allowed for any seller by the owner's choice (user decision). Every such sale stores the normal price next to the charged price; History flags it and the Item export has both columns. No approval step and no audit event — stated in tech debt. |
| Price or quantity tampering in the cart | The cart is persisted in `localStorage` and could be edited by someone with the device and developer tools, as before. `completeOrder` now refuses non-positive, non-finite or oversized quantities and non-integer or negative prices. Device-only enforcement remains the known limit (ADR-003/004). |
| Money precision | Line totals are integers by construction; "buy by amount" is exact for every tested price; totals reconcile (`subtotal − discounts + service + tax`). Refund of a weighed line returns exactly its share and the order can reach fully refunded. |
| Input parsing | Weight input accepts digits with one comma or dot only; anything else is rejected (no `eval`, no locale guessing). |
| Exports | New text column (unit) is a fixed symbol; numeric columns stay numeric. |
